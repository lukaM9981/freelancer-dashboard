**Card** — flat content surface. White fill with a hairline border *or* a soft warm shadow (not both heavy). 16px radius.

```jsx
<Card variant="bordered"><h3>How design systems accelerate product development</h3></Card>
<Card variant="accent"><Badge variant="brand">Case Study</Badge>…</Card>
<Card variant="inverse">Ink panel content</Card>
```

Variants: `default`/`bordered` (hairline), `elevated` (shadow), `accent` (yellow frame for case studies), `subtle` (sunken well), `inverse` (ink). See `StatCard` for big-number cards.
